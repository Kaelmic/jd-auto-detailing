COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you enquire.

What's the difference between a wash and a detail?

Detailing focuses on a more thorough cleaning and finishing process, including hard-to-reach areas and surface care.

Does ceramic coating remove scratches?

No. Coating is primarily a protective layer. Paint correction is a separate process intended to reduce certain visible surface defects.

How long does a detail take?

It depends on the vehicle's size, condition and chosen service. A real business would confirm an estimated duration during booking.

Can I request a combination of services?

Yes, use the enquiry form to describe what you need. This concept website does not accept real bookings.
