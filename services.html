COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you enquire.

What's the difference between a wash and a detail?

Detailing is a more thorough cleaning and finishing process, including hard-to-reach areas and surface care.

Does ceramic coating remove scratches?

No. Coating is a protective layer. Paint correction is a separate process that reduces certain visible surface defects.

How long does a detail take?

It depends on the vehicle's size, condition and the chosen service. We confirm an estimate when you enquire.

Can I request a combination of services?

Yes. Use the enquiry form to describe what you need. This concept website does not accept real bookings.
