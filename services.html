No packages with fancy names. Pick the job your car needs, and we will tell you straight if it needs something else.

01

Paint Correction

Takes out the swirl marks and haze that make good paint look tired.

Good for
Swirl marks in direct sun, light scratches, dull or hazy paint.
What happens
A test panel, staged machine polishing, a wipe-down and a final check under hard light.
Worth knowing
A scratch that catches your fingernail has gone through the clear coat. That needs paint, not polish.

02

Interior Detailing

The parts you touch every day, cleaned properly instead of wiped over.

Good for
Stained seats, grimy trim, pet hair, a cabin that never feels clean.
What happens
Vacuum, steam and extraction where the material allows, leather clean and conditioning, glass and screens.
Worth knowing
Some stains are in the fibre for good. We will tell you which ones before we start.
Enquire about this →
03

Ceramic Coating

A hard layer over the paint so it stays cleaner and is easier to wash.

Good for
Paint that is already in good shape, on a car you want to keep that way.
What happens
Full decontamination, correction where needed, the coating itself, and cure-time instructions.
Worth knowing
A coating protects. It does not repair. Scratches are dealt with first, never sealed in.
Enquire about this →
04

Exterior Detailing

A proper wash and finish for a car that has never had one.

Good for
A regular refresh, or a car that has only ever seen a drive-through wash.
What happens
Hand wash, iron and tar removal, clay, sealant, tyres and trim.
Worth knowing
It will not remove swirl marks. If that is the problem, start with paint correction.

Before you ask

The four questions we hear most.

What is the difference between a wash and a detail?

A wash removes dirt. A detail also deals with what a wash leaves behind: bonded contamination, swirls, dull trim, the gaps and edges. It takes hours, not minutes.

Does ceramic coating remove scratches?

No. A coating is a protective layer. Paint correction is a separate job that reduces the scratches first.

How long does a detail take?

It depends on the size and condition of the car and the job. We give you an estimate once we have seen it.

Can I combine services?

Yes, and it often makes sense, since correction comes before a coating. Say what you want in the enquiry form. This is a concept site, so no real bookings are taken.
