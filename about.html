Slow work. Straight answers.

JD Auto is a detailing studio for people who would rather know what was done to their car than be told it is "sorted". We take our time, and we write down what we find.

The order of work

Every car goes through the same five stages, in the same order. Skipping one is how a good polish ends up on a dirty panel.

  1. 01

    Inspect

    Before any water touches the car, we walk it under hard light and note what is there: swirls, etching, stone chips, worn trim. You get the list in plain words.

  2. 02

    Wash and decontaminate

    Hand wash, then iron remover and clay. Paint should feel like glass before it is polished, otherwise we are grinding dirt into it.

  3. 03

    Correct

    A test panel first. We use the lightest pad and polish that gets the result, because clear coat only has so much to give.

  4. 04

    Protect

    Wax, sealant or ceramic coating, chosen for how the car is driven and parked, not for what costs more.

  5. 05

    Hand over

    We walk around the car with you, show what changed and what did not, and explain how to wash it so it stays that way.

What we won't do

A short list, so you know where we stand before you book.

  • Promise every scratch will disappear.Deep ones need paint, not polish.
  • Polish through the clear coat for a perfect look.We measure, and we stop when there is nothing safe left to take.
  • Sell a coating to a car that needs a good wash.If that is all it needs, that is what we will say.
  • Rush a car to fill the schedule.Fewer cars a day is the whole point.
